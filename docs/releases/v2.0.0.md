# SoulsTracker v2.0.0

## Compatible games

- Demon's Souls
- Dark Souls Remastered
- Dark Souls II: Scholar of the First Sin
- Dark Souls III
- Bloodborne
- Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice
- Elden Ring
- Black Myth: Wukong
- Lies of P

## Latest changes

- SoulsTracker now focuses on total deaths, without boss lists or death sounds.
- Death Counter Overlays are now hosted instead of being local only.
- Save selection and reader status are clearer, with an appearance preview and optional startup update checks.
- Uninstall now offers a choice to keep or delete local SoulsTracker settings.
